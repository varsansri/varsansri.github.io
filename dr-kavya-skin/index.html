Meet Your Doctor

Dr. Kavya Sharma
MD Dermatology

"Every skin has a story. My role is to help you write the most radiant chapter of yours."

Dr. Kavya Sharma is a board-certified dermatologist with over a decade of expertise in medical and cosmetic dermatology. She completed her MBBS from Bangalore Medical College and earned her MD in Dermatology from Seth GS Medical College, Mumbai — one of India's most prestigious institutions.

Recognised as Bangalore's top-rated skin specialist, Dr. Kavya combines evidence-based medicine with a deeply personalised approach. She has successfully treated over 5,000 patients for conditions ranging from chronic acne to advanced laser procedures and anti-ageing therapies.
