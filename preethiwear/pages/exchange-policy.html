Exchange Policy

 

Exchange Policy

We aim to provide a hassle-free exchange experience to ensure customer satisfaction. Please review the guidelines below for our exchange process:

Customer Support

  • Office Hours:
    Monday to Saturday
    10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM to 6:00 PM

  • Customer Care Contacts:

    • Calls: 7418325657
    • WhatsApp: 9056656698
    • Email: support@preethiwear.in

  • Note:

    • Queries received outside working hours will be addressed on the next working day.
    • WhatsApp queries should only be sent to 9056656698.

Exchange Terms

  1. Eligibility for Exchange:

    • Exchange requests must be submitted within 3 days of delivery. Requests beyond 3 days will not be accepted.
    • Submit your exchange request via https://preethiwear.ecoreturns.ai/apps/ecoreturnsai.
    • For hygiene and safety reasons, intimate wear items are not eligible for exchange or return.
    • Each original order is eligible for only one exchange. Exchange requests for items that have already been exchanged once will not be accepted.
    • Products purchased under mega sale, clearance sale, Buy 1 Get 1, or similar promotional offers are not eligible for exchange or return.
    • Customized products are not eligible for exchange or return.
  2. Applicable Scenarios for Exchange:
Reason Courier Charges
Wrong item shipped ₹200 will be refunded after placing the exchange request.
Defective item received ₹200 will be refunded after placing the exchange request.
Size issues (large/small) ₹200 is charged for reverse pickup + reinitiating from the warehouse.
Product quality issue
₹200 is charged for reverse pickup + reinitiating from the warehouse.

Important Guidelines

  • Unpacking Video:

    • For damage or wrong item claims, a continuous unpacking video without cuts or edits is mandatory as proof.
    • The video must clearly show the product and the weight of the parcel.
    • Missing claims without a proper unpacking video will not be considered.

  • Reverse Pickup:

    • We will arrange reverse pickup at the customer’s convenience. The parcel must be handed over on the mentioned date and time.
    • Failing to return the parcel on the scheduled date will require manual return by the customer. Courier agents will not reattempt pickup.

  • Condition of Items:

    • Returned items must be unused, unwashed, and free from odors, with tags intact. Items failing quality checks will not be eligible for exchange.

Additional Notes

  1. Exchange is applicable across different categories.
  2. If the required replacement product is unavailable:
    • The customer will receive store credit equal to the product’s value.
    • Alternatively, the customer can choose another product from the available collection.
  3. Refund Policy:
    • Cash refunds are not provided for exchanges. Store credit will be issued instead.
  4. Exchange shipping will commence only after the original item passes the quality check.
  5. Once an exchange request is placed, changes to the selected items cannot be made. We kindly request customers to review and confirm their selections carefully before submitting the request.

For further clarifications, feel free to contact us at support@preethiwear.in.
