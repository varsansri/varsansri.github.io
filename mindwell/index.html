About Dr. Meera

A Doctor Who Listens With Her Heart

Dr. Meera Iyer is a Bangalore-based psychiatrist with over 12 years of experience helping people through anxiety, depression, relationship difficulties, trauma, and life transitions.

She founded MindWell on a simple belief: that healing begins when someone truly feels heard. Her sessions are warm, unhurried, and free from judgment — a space where you can say what you've never been able to say out loud.

Whether you're facing a crisis or simply feel stuck, Dr. Meera works alongside you — not above you — using evidence-based approaches tailored to your life, your culture, and your pace.

Our Philosophy

Why MindWell
is Different

We built MindWell around one question: "What would it take for someone in real pain to actually walk through that door?" Every detail of how we work comes from that answer.

A Truly Safe Space

No pressure, no diagnoses on day one, no rushing. Our sessions move at your pace. You are always in control of what you share and when.

Evidence-Based Methods

We use CBT, EMDR, mindfulness-based approaches, and psychodynamic therapy — not one-size-fits-all, but carefully chosen for you.

Complete Confidentiality

What you share stays here. We follow strict ethical and legal confidentiality standards. Your privacy is sacred — not a policy footnote.
