Our Story

Born in Bangalore,
Made for the World

Lumière was born from a singular belief — that Indian craftsmanship deserves the same reverence as any atelier in Paris or Milan. Founded in 2019 on the streets of Indiranagar, we set out to create fashion that tells a story.

Each piece begins with artisans from across Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and Rajasthan — weavers, block-printers, and embroiderers who have honed their craft across generations. We bring their hands together with the precision of modern couture.

The result is clothing that feels like an heirloom the moment it is worn. Slow fashion, made with intention.
