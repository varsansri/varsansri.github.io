About the Doctor

Dr. Rajesh Sharma

A trusted name in Bangalore's medical community, Dr. Sharma brings over 20 years of dedicated clinical practice to Indiranagar. His approach combines modern diagnostics with genuine patient-centred care.

Trained at St. John's Medical College, he holds specialisation in Internal Medicine and is a fellow of the Indian Medical Association. Fluent in Kannada, Tamil, Hindi and English.

MBBS — St. John's Medical College, Bangalore
