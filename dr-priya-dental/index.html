About Dr. Priya Menon

Crafting Beautiful Smiles Since 2009

A passion for dentistry, a gentle touch, and a commitment to the latest techniques — all in one clinic.

Dr. Priya Menon is a highly accomplished dental specialist with a BDS from Bangalore Medical College and an MDS in Orthodontics from Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences. With over 15 years of dedicated practice, she has transformed thousands of smiles through precision, artistry, and compassionate care.

Her clinic in Koramangala is equipped with digital X-rays, 3D imaging, and laser dentistry tools — ensuring painless treatments and faster recovery for every patient.
